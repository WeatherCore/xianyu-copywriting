# Description

## 中文版

xianyu-copywriting 是一个把自产学习笔记 PDF 写成闲鱼商品上架文案的 AI Skill：完整阅读铁律要求已读页数等于 PDF 总页数，杜绝卖点静默丢失；冻结骨架模板锁定黄金案例的段落结构与句式密度，产出与五篇真实案例同腔同构；事实反推规则要求数字实测反推、截图抽验，查无依据的背书不写；gate.py 机器体检对照五案例实测分布卡硬信号（方法签名串、XML 标签、直角引号、密度指标），出带即返工，交付消息附一行自检摘要；读不全的页明示报告绝不静默跳过，盗版转卖与代写代做直接拒绝。工程由 SKILL.md、五案例蒸馏风格指南、真实翻车案例拆解与体检脚本构成，适合把技术笔记、复习提纲、题解一键变成高密度商品详情页。

## English

xianyu-copywriting is an AI skill that turns your self-made study-note PDFs into high-density Xianyu (Goofish) marketplace listings — one listing that reads like a complete product page, distilled from five proven golden cases. Its guarantees are engineering constraints, not prompts: a full-reading iron rule (pages read must equal page count) so no selling point silently drops; a frozen skeleton template that locks paragraph order and sentence rhythm to the golden-case structure, eliminating density drift; a fact-derivation rule (numbers must be traceable to the PDF, screenshots spot-checked, unverifiable endorsements dropped); a `gate.py` machine gate that measures a draft against the real distribution of the five golden cases and hard-fails on API-outline tells (method-signature strings, XML tags, corner brackets, density outliers) with a one-line self-check summary; an explicit fallback for unreadable pages (visual reading, page-by-page reporting, never silent skips); and a hard refusal on pirated resale and ghostwriting. Composed of SKILL.md, a distilled style guide, a teardown of a real failed draft, and the checker script, it suits authors monetizing their own technical notes, revision outlines, and problem solutions.
