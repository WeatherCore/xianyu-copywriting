# Description

## 中文版

xianyu-copywriting 是一个为「学生自产学习资料」写闲鱼商品文案的 skills，把笔记、提纲、题解、模板写成同学之间安利的语气。它通过卖点挖掘十问（答不满 6 个不硬写，杜绝编造卖点）、五类油腻黑词库与商家腔对照表（逐词拦截喊麦腔）、量化语气红线（感叹号≤2、emoji≤3、≥3 个事实数字、必写一条局限）让真诚变成可验收的硬指标，平台红线（盗版转卖、代写代做、账号买卖、站外引流）触发即拒并内置合规声明模板。工程为纯 Markdown 渐进式披露架构，零运行时依赖，全部配方锚定一份真实旧文案的批注改写样例。适合想出摊卖自产学习资料、又不想像商家喊麦的学生卖家。

## English

xianyu-copywriting is a DeepSeek Harness skill that turns student-made study materials—notes, review sheets, problem sets, templates—into Goofish (Xianyu) listings that read like a classmate's recommendation instead of a storefront pitch. Its substance is enforceable honesty: a ten-question fact-mining checklist that forbids writing when fewer than six answers exist, a five-category cliché blacklist paired with a merchant-voice-to-classmate-voice rewrite table, and quantified tone gates (≤2 exclamation marks, ≤3 emojis, ≥3 verifiable numbers, at least one admitted limitation) checked before delivery. Platform red lines—pirated resales, ghostwriting, account trading, off-site funneling—are refused outright, with built-in compliance statement templates. Zero runtime dependencies, pure progressive-disclosure Markdown, and every recipe anchored to a real annotated rewrite sample. For students who want to sell their own study materials without sounding like a hawker.
